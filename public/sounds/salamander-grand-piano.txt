salamander-grand-piano.sf3

Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

SF2 assembly by roberto@zenvoid.org for the FreePats project:
https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html

Changes made for koimidi (scripts/build-piano.mjs in
https://github.com/MeRuslan/koimidi): 4 of the 16 velocity layers kept
and widened to cover all velocities, a milder velocity-to-volume curve,
sample tails shortened to 2.5–9 s with a fade-out, samples compressed
with Ogg Vorbis (SF3).
